# Resonant Sips - Gameplay Voiceover Guide (English)

This document is written for recording a gameplay introduction video with English voiceover.

---

## 1) One-Line Pitch

`Resonant Sips` is a cyberpunk narrative bartending game where you build trust through dialogue, read hidden emotions, and mix drinks that emotionally resonate with each guest.

---

## 2) Suggested Video Flow (with Voiceover Script)

### Segment A - Opening (0:00-0:20)

**Show on screen**
- Title screen / bar scene ambience
- UI at rest: day label, trust bar, chat panel, bartender station

**Voiceover**
> Welcome to Resonant Sips, a story-driven cyberpunk bartending game.  
> In this city, every guest wears an emotional mask.  
> Your job is to listen, read what they hide, and serve a drink that truly matches their state.

---

### Segment B - New Game Setup (0:20-0:45)

**Show on screen**
- Open New Game Setup
- Add/select a character ID
- Start game

**Voiceover**
> Before a run starts, you choose who can appear in your character pool.  
> The current setup uses single-select mode, so one active character ID drives the session.  
> Once selected, we enter the bar for Day One.

---

### Segment C - Conversation and Trust (0:45-1:30)

**Show on screen**
- Guest arrival
- Send 2-3 replies using quick options and typed input
- Trust bar changes

**Voiceover**
> Each round begins as a conversation.  
> You can reply with quick prompts or type your own line.  
> Trust is the core progression signal: better listening and better timing usually raise trust, and higher trust reveals deeper emotional clues.

---

### Segment D - Emotion Reading (1:30-2:00)

**Show on screen**
- Trigger emotion-guess step
- Select hidden emotions

**Voiceover**
> Guests usually show a surface emotion, but that is not always the truth.  
> Here, you infer what they are actually feeling underneath.  
> A correct emotional read unlocks the full mixing phase for that guest.

---

### Segment E - Mixing Gameplay (2:00-3:10)

**Show on screen**
- Enter mixing station
- Build a drink: glass -> ice -> ingredients -> garnish / decoration
- Show mixture values and target check
- Serve drink and show result card

**Voiceover**
> Mixing is built around three axes: Body, Sweetness, and Strength.  
> Every ingredient and add-on shifts these values in different ways.  
> You compose a drink step by step, then serve it and see whether it resonates.  
> A successful serve pushes the relationship forward. A miss gives feedback, and you can adapt in the next exchange.

---

### Segment F - Day Loop, Systems, and Narrative Depth (3:10-4:00)

**Show on screen**
- End-of-day modal
- Start next day
- Optional: return customer, chapter transition, atmosphere/event overlay

**Voiceover**
> The game runs in day cycles.  
> At day end, you review outcomes and move to the next day.  
> As you progress, systems expand: atmosphere modifiers, event chains, return customers, chapter transitions, memory fragments, and ending paths.  
> That means each run can evolve differently depending on how you talk, what you infer, and what you pour.

---

### Segment G - Closing (4:00-4:20)

**Show on screen**
- Calm bar shot, guest on stage, UI stable

**Voiceover**
> Resonant Sips is not about speed - it is about attention.  
> Listen well, read between lines, and mix with intention.  
> In this bar, flavor is language.

---

## 3) Core Rules (Condensed Summary)

- One run progresses through **days** and **guest encounters**.
- You interact in two linked phases:
  - **Dialogue / trust-building**
  - **Emotion reading + drink mixing**
- Mixing revolves around:
  - **Body**
  - **Sweetness**
  - **Strength**
- Guest outcomes affect:
  - trust flow
  - day results
  - long-form narrative systems (returns, chapters, endings)

---

## 4) Feature Highlights to Mention in Voiceover

- Narrative-first cyberpunk bar atmosphere
- AI-assisted guest dialogue generation
- Emotional mask vs hidden emotion gameplay
- Multi-step cocktail construction with readable stat impact
- Dynamic progression systems:
  - atmosphere effects
  - event chains
  - return-customer arcs
  - chapter-based mode shifts
  - endgame continuation options

---

## 5) Optional Alternative Intro (Short Version)

> Resonant Sips is a cyberpunk bartending narrative game.  
> You talk with guests, decode what they really feel, and craft drinks based on Body, Sweetness, and Strength.  
> Trust, resonance, and your day-by-day choices shape who returns, how stories branch, and how the bar remembers you.

---

## 6) Recording Tips

- Keep background music lower during dialogue clips so subtitles remain readable.
- Hold each UI transition for 1-2 seconds before narration line starts.
- If possible, record one clean successful serve and one failed serve for contrast.
- Capture at least one day transition and one narrative progression element (return guest, event, or chapter screen).
